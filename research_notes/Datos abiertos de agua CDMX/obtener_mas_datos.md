# How to get more Mexico City (SACMEX / SEGIAGUA) water-consumption data: routes, contacts, timelines (status 18 Oct 2026)

Note on names: SACMEX now operates as the **Secretaría de Gestión Integral del Agua (SEGIAGUA), "antes Sistema de Aguas de la Ciudad de México"**. INFO CDMX resolutions from 2025 name it that way, so any transparency request must go to SEGIAGUA. In the PNT it still answers under the old SACMEX folio prefix 0901735…. ([RR.IP.1412/2025](https://documentosobligaciones.infocdmx.org.mx/ST/LTAIPRC-2016-OT/Art133/Fr01/2025/T02/INFOCDMX.RR.IP.1412.2025.pdf); [RR.IP.1535/2025](https://documentosobligaciones.infocdmx.org.mx/ST/LTAIPRC-2016-OT/Art133/Fr01/2025/T02/INFOCDMX.RR.IP.1535.2025.pdf))

## Q1. Transparency route (PNT → SEGIAGUA's Unidad de Transparencia → transparency oversight body): deadlines, current oversight body, past requests

### Takeaway
- **Deadlines:** a PNT request to SEGIAGUA must still be answered within **9 business days, extendable once by 7** (16 at most). This comes from the new Ley de Transparencia para el Acceso y Socialización de la Información de la CDMX (in force since 14 Jul 2026).
- **No working appeal:** INFO CDMX was abolished. Appeals (recursos de revisión) have been suspended since 12 Dec 2025, and the replacement body (a sub-office of the Contraloría) is not yet working; it is expected in 2027. A poor answer therefore cannot be appealed before the paper deadline.
- **Precedent:** a 2025 PNT request asked for consumption by alcaldía, colonia, type of use and **manzana** for 2021–2024. SEGIAGUA delivered only **alcaldía × type-of-use** annual tables, and INFO CDMX confirmed that answer as lawful ("no obligation to process").

### Cited Findings
**The legal framework now in force**
- The decree creating the **"Ley de Transparencia para el Acceso y Socialización de la Información de la Ciudad de México"** was published in the Gaceta Oficial on 13 Jul 2026 and enters into force the day after publication. It repeals the 2016 Ley de Transparencia, Acceso a la Información Pública y Rendición de Cuentas (Transitorios 3rd and 4th). — [Gaceta Oficial, 13 Jul 2026 (copy on IECM site)](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **The new oversight body:** the new law defines the "Autoridad Garante Local" as an "órgano desconcentrado de la Secretaría de la Contraloría General … con autonomía técnica y operativa" (art. 2, fr. III). Its rules take effect only "una vez que comience su funcionamiento" (Transitorio 5th). — [Ley 2026](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **Congress vote:** Congress approved the law on 31 May 2026 (53–5). INFO CDMX has 180 days to transfer data and assets. The press does not give the new body a formal name; a photo caption in El Universal quotes deputy Víctor Hugo Romo calling it "Instituto de Transparencia para el Pueblo", but the law text uses only the generic term. — [La Razón](https://www.razon.com.mx/ciudad/2026/06/01/adios-al-info-cdmx-avala-el-congreso-nuevo-organo/); [El Universal](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/metropoli/publican-nuevas-leyes-de-transparencia-contraloria-asume-funciones-tras-desaparicion-del-info-cdmx/)
- **When the new body may start:** in a June 2026 interview, Contralora Nashieli Ramírez reportedly said the creation decree would be published before year-end, that start-up could take about a year, and that the 2027 budget would fund it. This comes from a search-result summary only; the article returned HTTP 403. — [La Jornada, 20 Jun 2026](https://www.jornada.com.mx/2026/06/20/capital/026n2cap)

**Deadlines for SEGIAGUA (sujeto obligado)**
- **Response deadline:** art. 157 says the answer "no podrá exceder de nueve días, contados a partir del día siguiente a la presentación". It may be extended "hasta por siete días más" for founded and motivated reasons, and the extension must be communicated before the deadline expires. Days are business days ("se entenderán como hábiles"). — [Ley 2026, arts. 151–157](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **Prevención (clarification request):** if the request is unclear, the agency must ask for clarification within 3 days. The requester then has 10 days to reply, or the request is treated as not filed. — [Ley 2026, art. 149](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **The PNT remains the channel:** requests filed on the PNT get an automatic folio (art. 150), and answers delivered through the PNT are valid (art. 146). — [Ley 2026](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **Access is free** (art. 14 and art. 168). — [Ley 2026](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **Rules that shape what you will receive:**
  - Art. 153: no obligation "a elaborar documentos". However, "En el caso de que la información solicitada consista en bases de datos se deberá privilegiar la entrega de la misma en formatos abiertos."
  - Art. 164: the duty "no comprende el procesamiento de la misma, ni el presentarla conforme al interés particular del solicitante".
  - Art. 152: if processing exceeds technical capacity, the agency may offer **consulta directa** (in-person inspection) instead.
  - Art. 162: if the agency claims the information does not exist, its Transparency Committee must formally confirm inexistence and may order the information generated.
  
  — [Ley 2026](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **Appeals under the new law:** an appeal is filed within 15 days (art. 180). The oversight body resolves it in 30 business days, extendable by 10 (art. 183). Compliance takes up to 10 days (art. 188). — [Ley 2026](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **For comparison, the 2016 law had the same 9 + 7 business days** (old art. 212), and appeals were due within 15 business days. — [INFO CDMX resolutions citing art. 212 (search summary)](https://documentosobligaciones.infocdmx.org.mx/ST/LTAIPRC-2016-OT/Art133/Fr01/2021/CCLLER/INFOCDMX-RR.IP.1001-2021.pdf)

**Appeals frozen**
- **INFO CDMX suspension:** INFO CDMX Acuerdo 4418/SO/03-12/2025 suspended, "a partir del 12 de diciembre del 2025", the receipt, processing, notification and follow-up of recursos de revisión (access to information and ARCO). It also suspended complaints (denuncias) and compliance with amparo rulings. — [Acuerdo 4418/SO/03-12/2025](https://portal.infocdmx.org.mx/sites/default/files/2025-12/A121Fr01_2025-T04_Acdo-2025-03-12-4418.pdf)
- **The suspension continues:** Transitorio 14th of the 2026 law keeps the suspension of deadlines from the 19 Dec 2025 constitutional decree "vigente" during the 180 business days allowed for transferring INFO CDMX files. Transitorio 16th says pending proceedings will be handled by the Contraloría's new body "conforme a la normatividad vigente al momento en que éste entre en funciones". Transitorio 13th says transparency functions "no podrán suspenderse ni interrumpirse", which is the basis for treating agencies' own response duties as continuing. — [Ley 2026, transitorios](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)
- **Federal regime is different and does not apply:** the federal body "Transparencia para el Pueblo" (SABG) has 20 + 10 business days, but that regime covers federal agencies, not CDMX ones. — [Cultura Colectiva](https://news.culturacolectiva.com/noticias/mexico/solicitud-de-informacion-publica-como-pedirla-federal/)

### Inferences
- **Deadline arithmetic for a PNT request filed Monday 19 Oct 2026 (business hours):**
  - Day 1 is Tue 20 Oct.
  - The 9-day deadline falls on **Fri 30 Oct 2026**.
  - With the 7-day extension, it falls on **Tue 10 Nov 2026**, or **Wed 11 Nov** if 2 Nov (Día de Muertos) is declared non-working.
  - Both 2022 precedents were answered at about the deadline, and the 2025 request appears to have used the full 9 + 7. Counting 14–18 Apr 2025 as Holy Week non-working days, 1 Apr → 30 Apr is exactly 16 business days. **Realistically, the answer will arrive 30 Oct – 11 Nov:** probably after the 9 Nov submission and before the 20–27 Nov revision window.
  - A prevención (clarification request) restarts the clock, so the request must be unambiguous.
- **What the answer will probably look like** if you ask for "consumption by manzana for 2019-2…2025": an alcaldía × use-type (doméstico/mixto/no doméstico) annual m³ table, delivered as PDF or images inside an oficio. The agency will cite "no obligación de procesar". The 2025 precedent confirms that INFO CDMX accepted exactly this.
- **What is plausible:** the 2019 open file proves SACMEX could already produce a manzana-level bimonthly extract from the Sistema Comercial (billed consumption, measured plus average). A request has a better chance if it:
  - asks for the **existing database or extract** "en el estado en que se encuentre";
  - cites art. 153 (databases in open formats);
  - names the exact structure of the published "Consumo Agua (1° Semestre 2019)" file and its data dictionary;
  - explicitly excludes personal data (aggregated per manzana, no cuenta or address);
  - covers one year per request (several small folios) to blunt the "procesamiento" objection;
  - asks, as a fallback, for colonia-level aggregates and for consulta directa.
  
  Even so, the probability of manzana-level microdata by 11 Nov is low, roughly 10–25% (judgement, not sourced). Colonia- or alcaldía-level aggregates are more likely.
- **No enforcement before the deadline:** a recurso de revisión filed now would sit unprocessed until the Contraloría's new body starts, possibly in 2027. The transparency route therefore has no enforcement teeth on the paper's timeline. It is still worth filing now because it may also help a later paper.
- **Zero-cost quick win:** the answer files for the earlier folios (090173525000627: alcaldía × use 2021–2024; 090173522000091: 2021 by alcaldía; 090173522001841: by colonia) should be viewable in the PNT's public search of answered requests without filing anything. I could not verify this access (see Gaps).

## Q2. Has SACMEX consumption data for years other than 2019-1 been published anywhere?

### Takeaway
I found no public release of SACMEX consumption data at manzana grain for any period other than the first half of 2019. Derivative colonia layers on the CDMX geoportal are also built from 2019 data. Multi-year data exist only as alcaldía-level aggregates in PNT answers (2021–2024) and in scattered press figures.

### Cited Findings

## Q4. Is there a formal "solicitud de apertura de datos" (dataset-opening request) mechanism on the CDMX portal?

### Takeaway
No. The current CKAN portal has no "request a dataset" form, and none of the expected phrases appear on its home or About pages. Opening datasets runs through ADIP's annual **Plan de Apertura**, which takes input from civil-society working groups. ADIP then asks the agencies for data "siguiendo los canales institucionales".

### Cited Findings
- **Home page:** has no link, form or option to request new datasets. Closest items: "Acerca de" and the "Sistema Unificado de Atención Ciudadana" (atencionciudadana.cdmx.gob.mx), which is a general citizen-services system. — [datos.cdmx.gob.mx](https://datos.cdmx.gob.mx/)
- **About page:** says updating the portal "requiere la participación de las instituciones responsables" and gives no request channel. — [datos.cdmx.gob.mx/about](https://datos.cdmx.gob.mx/about)
- **Plan de Apertura 2020 (ADIP, Jan 2020):**
  - Under art. 14 of the Ley de Operación e Innovación Digital, ADIP may request information from each entity.
  - The plan was built through a "Mesa de Trabajo de Datos Abiertos" with 15 civil-society organisations; their proposals were documented on Plaza Pública.
  - Phase 4 is "Solicitar, siguiendo los canales institucionales, la información a los Sujetos Obligados".
  - The plan includes a tab "de conjuntos de datos solicitados por Organizaciones de la Sociedad Civil".
  
  — [Plan de Apertura 2020](https://datos.cdmx.gob.mx/ca/dataset/0669261a-21fb-4992-9e4d-422001e5d497/resource/47b88be2-fced-4a98-80b6-57cf7e5e0cbe/download/plan-de-apertura-2020.pdf)
- **Legal basis:** the Ley de Operación e Innovación Digital (Gaceta 31 Dec 2018, last reform 9 Jan 2020) governs data management and open government. The SCJN upheld its arts. 16–17 and the data inventory calendar in April 2024. — [ADIP / Consejería (search summary)](https://data.consejeria.cdmx.gob.mx//images/leyes/leyes/LEY_DE_OPERACION_E_INNOVACION_DIGITAL_PARA_LA_CIUDAD_DE_MEXICO.pdf)
- **Under the 2026 transparency law,** agencies must "Promover la generación, la documentación y la publicación de la información en formatos abiertos" (art. 22, fr. VII). This is a general duty, not a citizen petition right. — [Ley 2026](https://iecm.mx/www/marconormativo/docs/LTAIPyRC_CDMX_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- An "apertura" request is informal: email datosabiertos@cdmx.gob.mx and/or infoadip@cdmx.gob.mx. It is stronger if a university letter is attached and SEGIAGUA is copied. It carries no legal deadline, and in practice it feeds the next annual opening plan. Expect months, not weeks.

## Q5. Which route could deliver data before 9 Nov 2026, and which only for a later paper?
